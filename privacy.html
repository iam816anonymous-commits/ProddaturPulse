Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Proddatur Pulse handles information you submit through its local interest survey. Proddatur Pulse is an independent demand-discovery project focused on Proddatur, Andhra Pradesh. The services and activities described on the survey are ideas being researched; submitting the form is not a booking or a guarantee that anything will launch.

1. Information we collect

Depending on what you choose to provide, the form may collect your name, mobile number, age group, area or locality, interests, likely usage timeline, willingness-to-pay range, questions or suggestions, consent to follow-up and optional consent to receive occasional updates.

Please do not submit passwords, identity documents, financial account details, precise home addresses or other sensitive information in the form.

2. Why we use it

  • To understand which local services, activities and communities residents are interested in.
  • To assess demand by interest category and general area.
  • To respond to questions or suggestions you submit.
  • To contact you about the interests you selected, where you have agreed to that contact.
  • To send occasional related launch updates only if you separately opt in.
  • To prevent duplicate registrations using the submitted mobile number.

3. Where information is stored

Form submissions are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a private Google Sheet controlled by the project operator. The sheet is not intended to be public or published to the web. Google processes and stores data under its own terms and privacy practices. The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

Do not rely on the public website as a place to view or edit submitted personal information. Access to the registration sheet is restricted to authorized account holders.

4. Sharing

We do not display registration details publicly or sell them. We do not intend to share identifiable registration data with local businesses or other third parties for their own marketing. Information may be processed by the hosting and storage providers needed to operate the website and registration system, or disclosed where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We intend to retain registration information while the local project remains active and the information is reasonably needed for demand research and follow-up. We will delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed for those purposes, subject to legitimate recordkeeping or legal requirements.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your registration by submitting a message through the survey form. Include the mobile number used to register and clearly state what you are requesting. We may need to verify that you control the number before acting on a request. We will review verified requests and respond through the contact details you supplied where appropriate.

6. Consent and updates

The required consent allows us to store your submission and contact you about the interests you selected. The optional updates checkbox is separate. You can ask us to stop optional updates or withdraw consent by sending a request through the form. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and some information may need to be retained where legally required.

7. Children

The current survey is intended for adults aged 18 and above. The registration form does not accept under-18 registrations.

8. Security and limitations

We use access restrictions on the private spreadsheet and basic validation and duplicate checks in the submission process. However, the public intake endpoint can be accessed by anyone and no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

9. Questions and privacy requests

Use this separate form to ask a question or request access to, correction of, or deletion of a previous registration. It does not create a second registration and is not blocked by the one-registration-per-mobile-number rule. Enter the same mobile number you used when registering. We may verify control of that number before acting. Do not send identity documents or sensitive information.

Your message is stored in a separate private Requests tab for the project operator to review.

Important: Proddatur Pulse is a local interest survey, not a live service provider, booking platform or payment service. A response to the survey does not create a contract or guarantee a service.
